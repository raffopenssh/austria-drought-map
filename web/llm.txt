## What this dataset is

GW Power: the groundwater status of Austria. The headline model is the
**Groundwater Status Index (GWI)** — one 0–1 value (good/watch/stressed) for
each of the 7,850 Katastralgemeinden (KGs), fusing:

  gwi_q_trend   30%  IDW 10-yr eHYD groundwater level trend (clamp(-t/0.5 m/dec))
  gwi_q_div     10%  5-yr precip-vs-level divergence (clamp(-d/1.5 sigma))
  gwi_q_use     15%  groundwater-body abstraction intensity, Wasserschatz 2021
                     (clamp(Nutzungsintensitaet/40%), WEI+ convention)
  gwi_q_nitrate 20%  IDW latest WISE-6 nitrate (clamp(no3/50 mg/L))
  gwi_q_wfd     10%  WISE WFD 2022 water-body status risk
  gwi_q_edo     15%  Copernicus EDO CDI mean 2012-2023 (clamp(cdi/2))

Weights renormalized over available components; categories good <0.30 <=
watch <0.50 <= stressed. gwi_estimated=1 flags KGs where the nearest-3
(<=30 km) fallback replaced the primary 12.5 km IDW radius. Full method:
the Methods modal on the map, and scripts/build_gw_index.py in the repo.

A legacy composite drought risk_score (per Gemeinde) plus per-point station
observations (groundwater levels, nitrate, hydropower, WFD sites) with full
annual histories are carried alongside.

KG and Gemeinde payloads also carry "groundwater_body" (Wasserschatz 2021:
available resource, sector water demand m3/a, abstraction intensity %,
population on the body, public-supply L/cap/d) and "population" (Statistik
Austria 2002-2026 per Gemeinde: total, growth, 65+ share; per-year series
on the Gemeinde endpoint).

Where relevant, KG payloads also carry "hydropower_influence": statistically
significant couplings between upstream hydro plant releases (ENTSO-E A73
per-unit generation, partly downscaled from the national feed by plant type &
capacity) and downstream groundwater wells near the KG, matched along the OSM
river network (partial R2 over a precipitation baseline, F-test p<0.01, above
a 60-day-shift placebo). Informational only — NOT part of the GWI.
Method: scripts/analyze_plant_downstream.py; data: web/data/plant_influence.json.

KG and Gemeinde payloads may also carry "cryosphere" — the melting watering
cans above the aquifers, also informational and NOT part of the GWI:
  glacier: upstream ice area & net loss (Mm3/yr) from ASTER dh/dt (Hugonnet et
    al. 2021) distributed in WGMS FoG 2026-02, matched by walking the directed
    OSM waterway network DOWNSTREAM from RGI 6.0 outlines; remaining volume via
    volume-area scaling (Bahr et al. 1997) -> depletion horizon; plus the share
    of the KG inside the 2 km glacier-fed river corridor and a peak-water/
    collapse projection (year_half_of_todays_melt, year_ice_effectively_gone).
    Austria-wide: 735 glaciers, 348 km2, 363 Mm3/yr net loss 2014-19 (~32% of
    national groundwater abstraction, arriving as river water).
  snow: SNOWGRID-CL v2 (GeoSphere Austria, 1 km, 1961-2026) snow water
    equivalent on 1 Apr (peak store) and 1 Jul (high-alpine residual): mean of
    the last 10 years, 1961-90 baseline, %/decade trend and a linear projection
    to 2050. The median KG has lost ~18% of its 1 Apr store per decade.
Findings: groundwater stations inside the glacier-fed corridor trend +0.068
m/decade above their nearest non-corridor neighbours (melt subsidy, perm
p<0.001) and glacier-fed gauges still gain flow (+2.3%/dec at ice >=10% of
catchment) while ice-free gauges lose 2.0%/dec.
Method: scripts/build_glacier_downstream.py, build_glacier_corridor.py,
build_snow_reservoir.py, analyze_glacier_contribution.py, analyze_glacier_gw.py;
data: web/data/glacier_context.json, glacier_corridor.{json,geojson}.
Gemeinde payloads include the full per-year snow and melt series; KG payloads
carry summaries only.

Granularity: MIXED — gwi_* per KG, legacy metrics per Gemeinde, stations per
point. Keys: official 5-char kg_code / gemeinde_code strings.

## Sibling-service integration endpoints (/llm/)

Implements the Austrian Cadastre "Integration Spec for Sibling Data Services"
(https://cadastre-process-api.exe.xyz/api/v1/docs/llm.txt?section=integration).

The gwi_* metrics are computed AT the requested KG's centroid (granularity
"kg"). Legacy drought metrics map UP from kg_code to gemeinde_code (resolved
through the canonical EDM register: data/kg_registry.json built from
/api/v1/lookup). No local fuzzy matching of place names.

We carry four POINT datasets — groundwater level stations, WISE-6 nitrate
stations, hydropower plants and WISE WFD water-quality sites — each snapped
ONCE to its Katastralgemeinde (and parcel) via the cadastre's POST
/api/v1/spatial/points (exact point-in-polygon). Those observations are
returned under 'points', grouped by KG, with each station's full annual
history and (when confident) its parcel_id. KG-level roll-ups (station
counts, mean GW trend, total MW, #at-risk) are folded into 'metrics'.

  GET /llm/kg/{kg_code}            drought/water-stress metrics for one KG
  GET /llm/kg/{kg_code}.json       (alias; JSON is the only format)
  GET /llm/kgs?codes=a,b,c         batch (<=500 codes) -> {"results":[...]}
  GET /llm/gemeinde/{code_or_name} per-Gemeinde payload; accepts the 5-digit
                                   gemeinde_code or an exact municipality name
                                   (case-insensitive; ambiguous names -> HTTP 300
                                   with candidates; unknown names are resolved
                                   through the canonical cadastre /api/v1/lookup)
  GET /llm/muni/{code_or_name}     (alias of /llm/gemeinde/)
  GET /llm/gemeinden?codes=a,b,c   batch (<=500 codes) -> {"results":[...]}
  GET /llm/point/{id}              one station/plant/site by id, e.g.
                                   gw:336446, no3:ATPG90100012, pp:12, wq:AT...
  GET /llm/manifest.json           coverage + metrics schema (crawl entry point)
  GET /llm/covered_kgs.json        array of every kg_code we can answer (7,850)
  GET /llm/covered_gemeinden.json  array of every gemeinde_code (2,117)

  Parcel-scale / viewport endpoints (Siedler AHEAD list GW-1..GW-8):
  GET /llm/point?lon=&lat=         water context AT a coordinate: gwi_* re-evaluated
                                   with the same IDW at the point, depth_to_gw_m_est
                                   (+depth_confidence, from live eHYD stations' ground
                                   elevation - level), aquifer_type, nearest_gw_station,
                                   nearest_no3_station, nearest_river (nearest gauge),
                                   groundwater_body, now{} block, kg_code + parcel_id
                                   (cadastre PiP, cached; input quantised to 4 dp).
                                   404 no_data outside Austria / no coverage.
  GET /llm/points?west&south&east&north[&categories=a,b&limit<=500&history=0|1]
                                   all snapped points inside a bbox, latest metrics only
                                   (history=1 adds series) -> {points[],count,truncated}
  GET /llm/protection?west&south&east&north[&limit<=500]
                                   drinking-water protection zones (Schutz-/Schongebiete)
                                   as WGS84 polygons {id,name,type,zone,state,geometry};
                                   Länder WIS/INSPIRE AM; coverage OÖ Stmk T Bgld W NÖ Vbg
  GET /llm/flowpath?lon=&lat=      downstream MERIT-Basins reach chain from the nearest
                                   reach to the Austrian border: reaches[{reach_id,
                                   stream_order,length_km,glacier_fed,river,gauge?}],
                                   total_km, exit{river,border_point,sea}, geometry
                                   (LineString <=3 KB). Chain follows the main stem;
                                   river names from eHYD gauges within 400 m. Backed by
                                   mghydro.com (CC BY-NC-SA), cached per 3-dp coordinate;
                                   503 + retry_after_s if upstream is down.
  GET /llm/gwi.json                all 7,850 KGs: {kgs:{kg_code:[gwi, cat_index]}}
                                   (~40 KB gzip; Cache-Control 1 d)
  GET /llm/parcel/{parcel_id}      e.g. /llm/parcel/63307-133/5 -> points snapped onto
                                   the parcel, parcel meta (cadastre), point context at
                                   the parcel centroid, water_body, now{}
  Query params on /llm/kg and /llm/kgs: ?fields=metrics,points,drought,now (comma list
  of top-level keys to keep; unit_glossary only when asked) and ?history=0.

  Extra blocks on /llm/kg (never replace existing keys):
  drought{}  Copernicus EDO CDI dekads 2012-2023 per Gemeinde: p_drought_year (share of
             years with >=1 month of mean class >= warning), worst_year, season_profile
             (12 x mean CDI class 0-3 per calendar month), events[{year,start_month,
             end_month,cdi_max,class watch|warning|alert}]. Recovery classes count as 0.
  now{}      latest measured state: eHYD live stations (daily cron), IDW at the KG
             centroid: as_of, gw_level_anomaly_sigma (vs. the stations' own day-of-year
             climatology, sigma from the p5-p95 band), gw_percentile_of_month, n_stations,
             trend_30d_cm, no3_latest_mg_l, status normal|low|very_low|high.
  history[]  additionally precip_mm (NASA POWER 0.5 deg cell, annual sum) and
             gw_level_anomaly_m (mean of stations <=12.5 km vs. their record mean).

All /llm/* JSON: CORS *, ETag + If-None-Match -> 304, gzip on request,
Cache-Control max-age 3600 (gwi.json/manifest 86400). Static /data/*.json.gz
likewise carry ETag/Last-Modified. No rate limit; be reasonable (<10 req/s).

The per-Gemeinde payload mirrors the per-KG shape, plus 'kg_codes' (all KGs in
the Gemeinde) and the union of snapped points across those KGs (each point
carries its 'kg_code').

All kg_code / gemeinde_code values are 5-character zero-padded strings.
404 with {"error":"no_data"} when a KG/Gemeinde is outside our coverage.

## Notes for consumers

- kg_code and gemeinde_code are different numbering systems; we never mix them.
  All codes are 5-character strings — keep leading zeros, do not cast to int.
- Codes are resolved through the cadastre API's /api/v1/lookup (BEV EDM /
  Statistik Austria), the shared source of truth — we maintain no private
  name/code table.
- Version via as_of / updated_at, not by renaming keys.

catchment (KG + Gemeinde payloads, informational, NOT a GWI component)
  The real upstream contributing area of the river that drains the KG, from the
  MERIT-Hydro DEM / MERIT-Basins, delineated on demand via mghydro.com
  (M. Heberger, delineator, CC BY-NC-SA) for all 640 eHYD river gauges and
  cross-checked against the officially published eHYD catchment size: median
  agreement 0.1%, 85% within 10%; catchments >25% off (tributary outlets that
  snap onto the mainstem at a confluence) are re-probed on a grid of nearby
  outlets and otherwise excluded. 7,627 of 7,850 KGs fall inside a verified
  catchment; the smallest containing basin is reported.
  Fields: outlet_gauge, area_km2 (+ official eHYD area & agreement %),
  mean_annual_river_flow_mio_m3, river_flow_trend_pct_per_decade,
  glacier{ice_area_km2_in_catchment, ice_share_of_catchment_pct,
  net_ice_loss_mio_m3_per_year, net_ice_loss_share_of_annual_flow_pct,
  depletion_years_at_current_rate} from RGI 6.0 outlines intersected with the
  polygon (not a network snap), snow{store_1apr_mio_m3_last10 vs _1961_1990,
  store_1apr_as_share_of_annual_flow_pct, swe trends, grid_coverage} from
  SNOWGRID-CL v2 cells inside the polygon, and nearest_glacier_fed_river_reach
  from MERIT-Basins downstream flow paths of all 735 glaciers (485 reaches in
  or near Austria; river distance from ice by Dijkstra over the reach graph).
  Caveats: SNOWGRID covers Austria only, so volumes use the in-country cell
  count and the snow share-of-flow ratio appears only when >=90% of the basin
  is inside the grid (excludes Danube/Rhine/upper Inn). "Snow as % of annual
  flow" is a store/flux ratio (how snow-dependent the basin is), not a runoff
  share. MERIT is a 90 m DEM product: it does not know karst, canals or
  inter-basin hydropower transfers.
  Findings (scripts/analyze_watershed_cryosphere.py): the 1 Apr snowpack holds a
  median 7% of a basin's annual flow (p90 26%, max 84% Zemmbach); the median
  basin has lost 61% of its 1961-90 store. Flow trends grade with ice:
  -2.2%/dec at ice-free gauges vs +2.1%/dec where ice >=5% of the catchment
  (+3.0pp, perm p<0.001). GW stations in snow-rich basins (1 Apr store >=20% of
  annual flow) fall at -0.13 m/dec vs -0.23 in rain-fed basins (+0.102,
  perm p<0.001) despite similar precipitation divergence. Over 23 independent
  basins the 1 Apr store is 4.9 km3 today vs 8.9 km3 in 1961-90: the 4.0 km3
  already lost is ~9x the annual net glacier ice loss.
  Data: web/data/watershed_context.json, merit_reach_kg.json,
  catchments.geojson, merit_reaches.geojson; build scripts
  fetch_watersheds.py, fix_missnapped_watersheds.py,
  fetch_glacier_flowpaths.py, build_watershed_context.py,
  build_merit_glacier_reaches.py.
