## What this dataset is

GW Power: the groundwater status of Austria. The headline model is the
**Groundwater Status Index (GWI)** — one 0–1 value (good/watch/stressed) for
each of the 7,850 Katastralgemeinden (KGs), fusing:

  gwi_q_trend   30%  IDW 10-yr eHYD groundwater level trend (clamp(-t/0.5 m/dec))
  gwi_q_div     10%  5-yr precip-vs-level divergence (clamp(-d/1.5 sigma))
  gwi_q_use     15%  groundwater-body abstraction intensity, Wasserschatz 2021
                     (clamp(Nutzungsintensitaet/40%), WEI+ convention)
  gwi_q_nitrate 20%  IDW latest WISE-6 nitrate (clamp(no3/50 mg/L))
  gwi_q_wfd     10%  WISE WFD 2022 water-body status risk
  gwi_q_edo     15%  Copernicus EDO CDI mean 2012-2023 (clamp(cdi/2))

Weights renormalized over available components; categories good <0.30 <=
watch <0.50 <= stressed. gwi_estimated=1 flags KGs where the nearest-3
(<=30 km) fallback replaced the primary 12.5 km IDW radius. Full method:
the Methods modal on the map, and scripts/build_gw_index.py in the repo.

A legacy composite drought risk_score (per Gemeinde) plus per-point station
observations (groundwater levels, nitrate, hydropower, WFD sites) with full
annual histories are carried alongside.

KG and Gemeinde payloads also carry "groundwater_body" (Wasserschatz 2021:
available resource, sector water demand m3/a, abstraction intensity %,
population on the body, public-supply L/cap/d) and "population" (Statistik
Austria 2002-2026 per Gemeinde: total, growth, 65+ share; per-year series
on the Gemeinde endpoint).

Where relevant, KG payloads also carry "hydropower_influence": statistically
significant couplings between upstream hydro plant releases (ENTSO-E A73
per-unit generation, partly downscaled from the national feed by plant type &
capacity) and downstream groundwater wells near the KG, matched along the OSM
river network (partial R2 over a precipitation baseline, F-test p<0.01, above
a 60-day-shift placebo). Informational only — NOT part of the GWI.
Method: scripts/analyze_plant_downstream.py; data: web/data/plant_influence.json.

Granularity: MIXED — gwi_* per KG, legacy metrics per Gemeinde, stations per
point. Keys: official 5-char kg_code / gemeinde_code strings.

## Sibling-service integration endpoints (/llm/)

Implements the Austrian Cadastre "Integration Spec for Sibling Data Services"
(https://cadastre-process-api.exe.xyz/api/v1/docs/llm.txt?section=integration).

The gwi_* metrics are computed AT the requested KG's centroid (granularity
"kg"). Legacy drought metrics map UP from kg_code to gemeinde_code (resolved
through the canonical EDM register: data/kg_registry.json built from
/api/v1/lookup). No local fuzzy matching of place names.

We carry four POINT datasets — groundwater level stations, WISE-6 nitrate
stations, hydropower plants and WISE WFD water-quality sites — each snapped
ONCE to its Katastralgemeinde (and parcel) via the cadastre's POST
/api/v1/spatial/points (exact point-in-polygon). Those observations are
returned under 'points', grouped by KG, with each station's full annual
history and (when confident) its parcel_id. KG-level roll-ups (station
counts, mean GW trend, total MW, #at-risk) are folded into 'metrics'.

  GET /llm/kg/{kg_code}            drought/water-stress metrics for one KG
  GET /llm/kg/{kg_code}.json       (alias; JSON is the only format)
  GET /llm/kgs?codes=a,b,c         batch (<=500 codes) -> {"results":[...]}
  GET /llm/gemeinde/{code_or_name} per-Gemeinde payload; accepts the 5-digit
                                   gemeinde_code or an exact municipality name
                                   (case-insensitive; ambiguous names -> HTTP 300
                                   with candidates; unknown names are resolved
                                   through the canonical cadastre /api/v1/lookup)
  GET /llm/muni/{code_or_name}     (alias of /llm/gemeinde/)
  GET /llm/gemeinden?codes=a,b,c   batch (<=500 codes) -> {"results":[...]}
  GET /llm/point/{id}              one station/plant/site by id, e.g.
                                   gw:336446, no3:ATPG90100012, pp:12, wq:AT...
  GET /llm/manifest.json           coverage + metrics schema (crawl entry point)
  GET /llm/covered_kgs.json        array of every kg_code we can answer (7,850)
  GET /llm/covered_gemeinden.json  array of every gemeinde_code (2,117)

The per-Gemeinde payload mirrors the per-KG shape, plus 'kg_codes' (all KGs in
the Gemeinde) and the union of snapped points across those KGs (each point
carries its 'kg_code').

All kg_code / gemeinde_code values are 5-character zero-padded strings.
404 with {"error":"no_data"} when a KG/Gemeinde is outside our coverage.

## Notes for consumers

- kg_code and gemeinde_code are different numbering systems; we never mix them.
  All codes are 5-character strings — keep leading zeros, do not cast to int.
- Codes are resolved through the cadastre API's /api/v1/lookup (BEV EDM /
  Statistik Austria), the shared source of truth — we maintain no private
  name/code table.
- Version via as_of / updated_at, not by renaming keys.
